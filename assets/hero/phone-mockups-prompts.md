# CheckYaar transparent phone mockups

These early concepts were created with the built-in image-generation tool. Their generated screen content, brand colors and logo are interpretations. The final website uses separate `phone-*-real-ui.png` assets with actual app screenshots composited into the approved phone frame. PNG transparency was verified.

## Shared direction

Use case: product-mockup. Create an exceptional, photorealistic premium smartphone product rendering for CheckYaar, a Persian RTL rental-property handover app. Deliver ONE isolated complete phone on genuine transparent alpha, portrait canvas 1536x2048. Phone occupies about 85% of height, centered with comfortable transparent margin, all corners visible. No background of any color, no floor, no backdrop, no external shadows, no halo, no decorative shapes, no orbit, no particles, no floating cards, no external documents, no text outside the screen, no watermark. External PDF and approval badges will be implemented separately in code. Device: elegant ultra-slim graphite titanium smartphone with precision machined subtly rounded chamfered frame, extremely thin even black bezels, sophisticated edge highlights, tiny dark pill camera cutout, realistic side buttons, restrained glass reflections that never obscure the screen. Near frontal three-quarter view, subtle 8-degree counterclockwise roll and 10-degree yaw showing right metal edge, minimal perspective distortion; ambitious polished launch-campaign quality without theatrical effects. The beautiful screen is the hero, with sharp readable Persian Vazirmatn-style typography and generous whitespace. Physically plausible elongated handset proportions, not tablet proportions. Screen app UI uses EXACT flat color tokens from repository: accent #007AFF, canvas #F2F2F7, surfaces #FFFFFF, primary text #1C1C1E, secondary text #8E8E93, success #34C759, danger #FF3B30, pale blue #007AFF1F. No purple/cyan brand shifts. Keep these UI fills ungraded and true to the hex values. Reproduce the actual supplied blue square white house/check logo faithfully as a small header brand mark, do not invent or restyle logo. All content inside screen is opaque; only outside handset is alpha. Natural contemporary Persian RTL UI, coherent icons, absolutely no missing-glyph/tofu squares from test screenshots. No dates needed. Focus on one feature per phone; avoid crowded feature inventory.

Below it, a clear heading «بخش‌های ملک», small progress line «۷ از ۱۰ بخش», a thin right-to-left blue progress bar at 70%. Three clean grouped rows with real line icons: «پذیرایی» with small green «سالم», «آشپزخانه» with small red «ایراد دارد», «اتاق خواب» with small green «سالم».
